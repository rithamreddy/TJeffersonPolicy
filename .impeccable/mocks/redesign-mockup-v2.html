What policy debate actually is

Two-on-two, one national resolution for the whole year, and every claim backed by evidence you cut yourself.

Affirmative & negative
Each round, one team defends a policy change while the other argues against it. Teams switch sides each round.
One topic, all year
A single national resolution is debated all season, so debaters build real expertise in healthcare, defense, energy, and more.
Evidence first
Arguments are backed by evidence cut from academic journals, government reports, and the news. It's real research.
Think on your feet
Debaters answer complex arguments and build rebuttals in real time, under competitive pressure.
